# 🪨 Rock Paper Scissors ✂️

A fun and interactive Rock Paper Scissors game you can play right in your browser!

!

---

## 📝 Description

This project is a browser-based Rock Paper Scissors game. Click on rock, paper, or scissors to play against the computer. Your wins, losses, and ties are tracked and displayed after every round. Scores are saved in your browser, so you can come back anytime. Reset your score whenever you want with a single click!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Choose your move:** Click the 🪨, 📄, or ✂️ button.
2. **See the result:** The computer will randomly pick its move.
3. **Track your score:** Wins, losses, and ties are shown below the buttons.
4. **Reset anytime:** Click "Reset Score" to start over.

---

## 🖥️ Features

- Simple, modern interface with emoji icons
- Score tracking with local storage
- Instant feedback after each round
- Reset button to clear your score

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone or download this repository.**
2. **Open `index.html` in your browser.**
3. **Enjoy playing!**
